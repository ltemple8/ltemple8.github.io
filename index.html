Welcome!

I love libraries!

One of the very first things I did after moving to Brooklyn was get my Brooklyn Public Library (BPL) library card. I chose the card featuring an illustration from Maurice Sendak’s children's book Where the Wild Things Are. I am so excited to explore all the different branches and research libraries of the BPL!!

On this website you will find the following:

  • Historical information about the BPL
  • A list of all BPL branches
  • Informational pages for the Central and Macon branches
  • Resources to learn more about the BPL
  • Credits for sources used in the creation of this site
